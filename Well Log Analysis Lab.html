All logs are forward-modelled from a synthetic formation with known lithology, porosity, fluids, clay and invasion, using standard tool responses (gamma-ray K/U/Th, SP electrochemistry, invasion and resistivity geometry, density, neutron and sonic responses, Archie and shaly-sand saturation, NMR T2 relaxation). Values are realistic but simplified for teaching; chart-book corrections are approximated.
